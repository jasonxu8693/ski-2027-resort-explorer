Stop 1 · Résidence des Balcons, 2,300 m

Our apartment

Stop 3 · Ski school and the lift pass

Lessons and lift passes

Three schools, all with groups capped at eight. Audacius meets on the beginner carpets near us, ESF is the biggest and the cheapest, and Oxyski is English-speaking. For ten of us, a private morning for the whole group can cost less than ten group places. Lift passes go on sale online in October; buy them there and collect from the machines when we arrive.

  • ESF6 mornings €270 (~£229)
  • Audacius6 mornings €298–437 (~£253–370) by season band
  • Private, 4–10€299–407 (~£253–345) per 3-hour morning
  • Free first daysthe Musaraigne, Campagnols and Castor & Pollux carpets cost nothing
  • Book lessons bymid-November
  • 3 Vallées pass6 days €421 (~£357) for 2026/27; covers Méribel, Courchevel and Orelle
  • Val Thorens pass6 days €367.50 (~£312), Val Thorens and Orelle only; add a 3 Vallées day at the ticket office for €51 (~£43)
  • Which onebeginners staying local save £45 on the Val Thorens pass; anyone crossing to Méribel or Courchevel more than once wants the 3 Vallées
  • InsuranceCarré Neige, €3.50 a day, added when you buy the pass; covers piste rescue, which is not free in France

Stop 4 · Plein Sud and the snow front

Après, lunch and dinner

The Plein Sud chair beside the residence goes straight up to La Folie Douce: DJs and cabaret on the terrace from lunchtime, and La Frui­tière for the one nicer lunch of the week. Back in the village, Le Rendez-Vous on the snow front takes a table of ten, and Les Chalets du Thorens is the easy mountain lunch.

  • Folie DoucePlein Sud or Pionniers chair; ski pass needed; over-18s on the terrace from 2 pm; ski home before 5 pm
  • La Frui­tièresemi-gastronomic, at the Folie Douce; book separately
  • Le Rendez-Vouswood-fired pizza, pasta, Savoyard; 11:30–15:00 and 18:30–22:00; book for ten
  • Chalets du Thorenspizza, sushi, crêpes and a self-service at the foot of the Moraine gondola

Stop 5 · Cime Caron, sledges and zip lines

Fun on the mountain

Not everything is skiing. Ride to the top of the Cime Caron at 3,200 m, sledge France’s longest toboggan run by head torch, pile ten of us onto one Snake Gliss train once the lifts shut, and fly back into the resort on La Bee.

  • Cime Caronglass-floored footbridge and Caron 3200 at the summit; last lift up about 15:45; every way down is red or black, so beginners ride back down
  • Cosmojet6 km sledge from the foot of the Péclet glacier, about 45 minutes; €21 (~£18) a run by day, sledge, helmet and Funitel included, no booking
  • Cosmojet by nightWednesday to Friday, 16:45–18:00 in January; €31 (~£26) with a head torch; book the same day at the Chalet Cosmojet; runs only if 40 people sign up
  • Snake Glissup to ten sledges roped into one train, steered by a guide down the Tête Ronde and Bleuet runs after the lifts close; Tuesday to Friday at 17:00, weekends 16:00; €30 (~£25), ski pass needed, no ski boots, mulled wine at the bottom
  • La Bee1.8 km double zip line, up to 65 m off the ground, from the top of the 2 Lacs gondola into the resort; €30 (~£25); 50–120 kg; daily 09:45–16:00
  • Snow tubingMoon Xperience beside the Campagnols carpet; €12 (~£10) for 30 minutes; open to 18:45 Wednesday to Friday
  • Pixel Areathe fun park at Les Menuires, off the Sunny Express or Becca chair: kickers from XS to L, rails and a boardercross, good for the five of us on boards
  • BundlesCosmojet and tubing €27; add La Bee for €56 (~£47)

Stop 6 · The rest of the 3 Vallées

Beyond Val Thorens

Our pass covers the whole of the 3 Vallées. Méribel is the easy crossing for everyone, Courchevel is a full day for the confident, and Orelle is for the strong skiers. Every crossing has an afternoon cut-off, so turn for home by 15:30.

  • Méribelblue: up to the Col de la Chambre, down the Lac de la Chambre to Mottaret; home by two gondolas, last lift back about 16:25
  • Courchevelover La Saulire, four lifts each way; La Folie Douce Méribel-Courchevel halfway up, 09:00–17:00, last lift down 16:55
  • Orelleblack: down the Combe Rosaël off the back of the Cime Caron; strong skiers only
  • Photo framesa dozen giant frames at the viewpoints — La Saulire, the Pointe de la Masse, the Cime Caron
  • The ruleturn for home by 15:30

    Most whole apartments sold on booking.com and VRBO are non classé, which triggers the 5% proportional rate. The gap between best and worst case on the French week is €246 across the group. Ask the owner for the classification in writing.     Flight traps · verified

    • BA London City → Innsbruck stops 9 January 2027. Runs 12 Dec – 9 Jan only. Does not operate on Sat 16 Jan.
    • Luton → Innsbruck is Sunday-only. Useless for a Saturday changeover.
    • Ski carriage differs structurally. BA absorbs skis into the checked allowance; easyJet and Jet2 charge for skis on top of a hold bag with no weight pooling.

    Saturday Innsbruck arrivals that do work: BA2618 LGW 08:25→11:25 · easyJet EZY8693 LGW 07:00→09:55 · BA0686 LHR 10:00→13:05.

    Two operational moves worth making

    • Book the Val Thorens transfer private and request the Moûtiers supermarket stop at booking time. Ski-Lifts permits stops on private transfers only, with advance notice. Resort shelf prices run 10–30% higher.
    • Sherpa closes 12:30–16:00 and keys hand over "at the latest 5pm". A Saturday afternoon arrival can land with no shop open — the valley stop is the reliable fix for night one.

    No group discount exists at either resort

    Les 3 Vallées “Family Flex”max 2 adults, needs 1 child — you do not qualify
    Ski Arlberg groups1 free per 20 paying, company/club letterhead — you do not qualify
    Hire shopsno published adult group rate at Intersport or Sport 2000

    Do not budget a group discount. The only real cross-sell found: Audacius (the ski school formerly called Prosneige) gives 15% off kit if you book lessons with them.
